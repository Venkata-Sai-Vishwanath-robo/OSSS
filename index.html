Security system division

CCTV surveillance, from the camera to the command centre.

We are a distribution partner for Indian and global OEMs across the CCTV ecosystem, with a network spanning enterprise, government and industrial clients. Reliability, scalability and technology integration are what we are engaged for.

India's infrastructure growth, smart city programmes and rising safety expectations changed what a surveillance system has to do. Recording footage is no longer the job. Detecting the event, classifying it, and getting it in front of an operator in time is the job.

So we deliver the whole chain rather than a box: camera selection and positioning, cabling and power, NVR or server based recording, video management software, analytics tuned on your site, and the operator training to run it. One accountable team through survey, installation, commissioning and long term support.

Security system division

Access control and identity at every door.

A leading distribution partner for Indian and multinational OEMs in access control, with deep expertise in physical security and identity management. Trusted by enterprise, government and infrastructure clients.

We start with the door schedule: every controlled opening, who may pass through it, and when. That schedule drives the hardware rather than the other way round, because a stores door and a server room door are not the same problem.

From there we supply and integrate controllers, readers and credentials, standalone or network based, and connect the result to your CCTV so a door event and its footage are one record instead of two.

Entry and exitComplete management of controlled openings across a site or an estate
WorkforceAttendance, shift and zone permissions drawn from the same credential staff already carry
VisitorsPasses issued against a host with a validity window, expiring on their own
ArchitectureStandalone units for a single door, network based systems for multi-site estates
IntegrationSeamless connection into CCTV and the wider security ecosystem

Skill development division

The business we started with, and still run.

Founded in 2011, OSSS has over a decade of proven expertise in skill development and technical workforce training across India, having empowered thousands of individuals with vocational and technical skills and bridged the gap between unorganised labour and structured employment.

More than 55 dedicated staff and trainers move from place to place to train unorganised workers, make them skilled, and bring them into organised sections of society. We also train young adults in employability skills such as computers and communication so they can be placed in industry and services.

Headquartered in Hyderabad, OSSS operates offices and training branches in Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, Delhi, Maharashtra, Haryana and Karnataka, with more than 15 training centres across various cities depending on mandate and demand. Centres are equipped with the kits and instruments the trade requires, and courseware is generally prepared by our in-house experts.

OSSS works closely with Indian government agencies, ministries, and financial and regulatory bodies, and with state governments, agencies and corporates to identify skill gaps and address them with programmes matched to industry demand. A team of specialists researches best practice nationally and internationally and provides inputs to policy makers, corporates and NGOs.

EntrepreneurshipToday we need more employers than employees. We train young men and women in entrepreneurship so they can be self employed, supported by Indian nationalised banks and the Mudra bank to start micro, small and medium enterprises.
Skills upgradationTrade specific upgradation for workers already in the field, delivered on site or at our centres using in-house courseware and hands-on kits.
Work place safetySafety is a key factor in the training world, and OSSS includes safety aspects in the courseware of every programme rather than treating it as an optional module.
CSR deliveryWe can train people in the areas specified by your CSR board and let you monitor progress throughout. If your CSR helps someone acquire a livelihood, that person will be grateful to you for a long time.

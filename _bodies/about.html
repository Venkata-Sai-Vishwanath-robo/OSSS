About us

Security systems and skill development, from Hyderabad since 2011.

Oriental Skills & Safety Services Pvt Ltd began as a training company. We now run two divisions: electronic security systems, and vocational and technical training. Both operate from the same office and to the same standards.

Company profile

What we do and where we work.

OSSS is a private limited company registered in Hyderabad. The head office is at Moula Ali, with offices and training branches in Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, Delhi, Maharashtra, Haryana and Karnataka.

The security division supplies, installs, commissions and maintains CCTV and access control systems. As a distribution partner for Indian and international manufacturers, we specify equipment according to the requirements of the site rather than a single brand's catalogue.

The training division has been running since 2011. Our trainers travel to where the work is, and most of the courseware is written by our own specialists. It is also how we staff the security division with engineers we have trained ourselves.

Our history

From training to security systems.

The company was founded to run training programmes. The security division followed later, and both continue side by side.

2011

Company founded

OSSS is established in Hyderabad by a group of engineers, retired civil servants, diplomats and policy advisers, to run vocational and technical training.

2012 – 2019

Expansion across states

Branches and training centres open across Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, Delhi, Maharashtra, Haryana and Karnataka, with courseware written in house.

2020 – 2021

Government programmes

Programmes are run with ministries, sector skill councils, state agencies and corporate CSR boards, matched to the skill gaps those bodies identify.

2022

Security systems division formed

OSSS enters CCTV and access control, taking on turnkey work for Indian Railways, state government departments and industry.

Today

Both divisions in operation

Systems in service across railway divisions, government schools, highways and defence sites, maintained by our own engineers.

Vision & mission

What we are working towards.

To be recognised as a dependable provider of electronic security systems and skill development services in India, supporting safety, technology and employment.

01

Reliable systems

To deliver technically sound security systems for India's growing infrastructure, and to keep them running after installation.

02

Lasting partnerships

To build long term relationships with Indian and international manufacturers, so that clients receive support years after installation.

03

Skilled manpower

To strengthen safety and surveillance infrastructure in India through trained manpower as well as through technology.

04

Productive training

To run training that improves what an individual can earn and adds to the productivity of the state and the national economy.

Why work with us

Six reasons clients appoint us.

These are the points clients raise most often when explaining why they continue to work with us.

Infrastructure, supply, installation, integration and support are delivered by us as a single scope of work, so there is no need to coordinate a cabling contractor, a hardware vendor and a software integrator separately.

Certified engineers and technicians on our payroll, specialising in CCTV and access control. They are trained through our own programmes and retrained as camera, analytics and credential technology develops.

Offices and deployment-ready teams across Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, Delhi, Maharashtra, Haryana and Karnataka, so mobilising to a site does not depend on long-distance travel.

We distribute for several Indian and international manufacturers, which allows cameras, readers and recording to be matched to the conditions on your site: light levels, weather, distance and the required retention period.

Most clients add to their systems later. We size the network, the recording and the licences so that a second phase is an addition to the installation rather than a replacement of it.

We work with Indian camera manufacturers to supply locally developed equipment for national infrastructure, industrial sites, smart cities and public utilities. For public sector buyers this assists both with local content requirements and with the availability of spares and firmware support from a manufacturer within the country.

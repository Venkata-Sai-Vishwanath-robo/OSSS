How we work

Every project begins with a site survey.

We visit the premises before issuing a quotation. It is the only reliable way to establish camera positions, cable routes and door hardware.

Book a survey

We visit the premises, record every entry point, mark camera positions and note what is already installed. You receive a written scope of work.

We select equipment to suit the site rather than a standard package, size the recording to your retention period, and issue one quotation with drawings.

Cabling, power, mounting, network and hardware, carried out by our own engineers. Where a site cannot be shut down, we plan the work around your operating hours.

Cameras focused, analytics configured against your own camera views, users and permissions set up, and your operators trained on the system before handover.

Annual maintenance contracts with scheduled service visits and call-out response. We also take over the maintenance of systems installed by others.

What happens next

From your enquiry to a quotation.

This is the sequence we follow for every enquiry.

01

We reply

Usually within one working day, to understand the site and confirm whether a survey is worthwhile for both parties.

02

We visit

An engineer walks the premises, records entry points, cable routes and existing equipment, and takes measurements.

03

Scope of work

A written scope with drawings and an equipment list, together with the reasoning behind each selection.

04

Quotation

One price covering supply, installation and commissioning, with the maintenance terms that follow handover.

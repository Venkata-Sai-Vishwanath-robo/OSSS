CCTV surveillance

Complete surveillance systems, from survey to maintenance.

We design systems in which an incident is detected, brought to an operator's attention, and can be retrieved from the recording afterwards.

We are a distribution partner for Indian and international manufacturers across the CCTV range, so equipment can be selected to suit the site. Light levels, weather, distance, mounting and the required retention period all affect what should be installed.

Because we supply the complete chain — camera selection and positioning, cabling and power, recording, video management software, analytics configured for your site, and operator training — one team remains responsible throughout.

Most of our surveillance work continues under an annual maintenance contract after handover, including systems originally installed by others.

Access control

Identity and entry control at every controlled door.

We begin with the door schedule: which openings are controlled, who may pass through them, and at what hours. The schedule determines the hardware, since a stores door and a server room door have different requirements.

We then supply and install the controllers, readers and credentials. A single door can run on a standalone unit; an estate with several buildings needs a networked system with one place to add and remove people.

Access control is connected to your cameras, so a door event and the footage of it form a single record rather than two that have to be matched up afterwards.

The credential your staff already carry can also cover attendance, shift timing and zone permissions, which avoids running three separate systems.

Analytics & control room

Cameras and access control in one system.

This layer joins the two systems together. The cameras record what happened and where; the readers record who was present and when. Held in one system, both are available from a single search.

What this means in practice

A door forced at 02:15 brings up the footage of that door either side of the event, together with the last credential used on it.

A vehicle at the gate is read by the number plate camera and checked against the same permission list the readers use, so one list governs both people and vehicles.

An area that should be empty after a shift ends raises an alert when movement is detected there, because no one has badged in.

And because it is a single system, the operator works from one screen during an incident rather than switching between two applications.

After handover

Annual maintenance contracts.

Cameras drift out of focus, lenses collect dust, and recording disks fill up. Scheduled service visits catch these during a routine check rather than when footage is needed.

We also take over the maintenance of systems installed by others. That begins with a survey of the existing installation and a written note of what needs replacing.

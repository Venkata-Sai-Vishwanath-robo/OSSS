How we run programmes

Our trainers travel to where the work is.

Our staff and trainers move between sites to train workers who have had no formal instruction, certify the skills they already have, and help them into organised employment. We also train young adults in computer and communication skills for placement in industry and services.

The head office is in Hyderabad, with offices and training branches in Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, Delhi, Maharashtra, Haryana and Karnataka, and training centres in several cities depending on the mandate. Centres are equipped with the kits and instruments each trade requires, and the courseware is generally prepared by our own specialists.

We work with government agencies, ministries, financial and regulatory bodies, state governments and companies to identify skill gaps and design programmes against them. Our specialists also study practice elsewhere in India and abroad and provide inputs to policy makers, companies and NGOs.

Programmes

Four types of programme.

Most requirements fall into one of these. If yours does not, tell us what you need and we will confirm whether we can deliver it.

01

Skills upgrading

Trade specific training for workers already in the field, delivered on your site or at our centres, using our own courseware and hands-on kits.

02

Entrepreneurship

Training for young men and women to become self employed, supported by Indian nationalised banks and Mudra loans for starting micro, small and medium enterprises.

03

Workplace safety

Safety is included in the courseware of every programme we run rather than offered as a separate module.

04

CSR programmes

Training delivered in the areas your CSR board nominates, with progress reported throughout the programme.

Who we run programmes for

Government schemes, companies and CSR boards.

Each type of sponsor requires something different from a programme: certification and placement figures for a ministry, improved performance on the job for a company, and clear reporting for a CSR board. We plan the programme accordingly.

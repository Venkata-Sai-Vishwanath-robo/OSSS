Our approach

Four things that keep a system working.

These are the points that determine whether an installation is still useful two years after handover. We build them into every project.

Request a survey

A system that no one is responsible for watching is also a system that no one maintains. At handover we set up permissions and shift responsibilities so that it is clear who is monitoring and who to contact.

Analytics left at factory defaults will alert on a moving branch or a passing animal, and operators soon stop responding. We configure the rules against your own camera views before handover.

Recording is only useful for as long as it is retained. We size storage against the retention period you are required to hold, rather than the smallest disk that fits the quotation.

Three years on, a camera fails and the model is discontinued. We favour equipment lines with long support periods, including Indian manufacturers, so that a replacement matches what is already installed.
